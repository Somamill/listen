# listen. repository artwork

Two original brand illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then copied into this repository. They are conceptual artwork, not screenshots or evidence of additional app features. README uses the light or dark version according to the viewer’s color scheme. No real book covers, reading history, account information, third-party logos, or ratings were used.

- [Light README hero](listen-macos-audiobook-discovery-hero.png)
- [Dark README hero](listen-audiobook-discovery-dark.png)

## Final prompt: light hero

Create a premium wide landscape 2:1 GitHub README hero illustration for the real macOS audiobook discovery app named "listen.". Art direction: quiet editorial minimalism, warm ivory paper background, sage and deep forest green, a restrained amber highlight, luminous translucent glass. Composition: generous left third negative space with the exact wordmark "listen." large, elegant contemporary serif, and ONLY the smaller subtitle "Audiobook discovery for Mac". On the right, sculptural translucent glass book shapes floating above a frosted rounded tile, a precise circle with its lower half filled (the symbol ◒) as a tasteful central brand sculpture, and a very subtle audio waveform. Sophisticated studio-rendered illustration, soft natural shadows, crisp edges, rich materials, polished open-source product branding. No people, no real book titles or book-cover artwork, no third-party logos, no screenshots, no invented app interface, no ratings, no extra text or watermarks. Keep all important text well inset from every edge. This is a brand illustration, not a product screenshot.

## Final prompt: dark hero

Create a wide landscape 2:1 premium editorial brand illustration for the macOS audiobook discovery app "listen.". A sophisticated dark counterpart to an ivory-and-sage product brand: deep forest-green almost-black background, translucent smoky sage glass, warm amber light, quiet cinematic studio shadows. On the right two-thirds, a beautiful sculptural glass bookshelf with three distinct blank glass book objects, restrained soft audio-wave contours, and the brand's precise circle with its lower half filled (◒) in frosted glass. No real book covers or titles. On the left place the exact elegant serif headline "Your next chapter." and a small exact "listen." wordmark below. Generous safe margins, polished magazine-quality 3D art, clear readable typography, calm and inviting rather than futuristic neon. No interface mockups or device screens, no third-party logos, no people, no invented feature labels, no badges, no ratings, no watermark. This is conceptual brand artwork for a GitHub README, not a screenshot. Match a tasteful macOS glass-inspired reading app.
