Your torrent client

Direct integrations, or hand off to the app you already love.

Enable the client’s Web/RPC interface first. Remote clients need paths on the remote machine. Use HTTPS for credentials sent across untrusted networks. Passwords are kept in the system credential store, never in repository files.

Magnet handoff works with any installed magnet-capable client. Its own settings control the destination, connection privacy, and download status; listen. cannot report its live health.

A home for your books

On your Mac, a server, or your cloud drive.

Per-download staging is supported with a local qBittorrent or Deluge client. For Transmission, configure its incomplete folder or switch staging off here. Books finish in your local downloads folder. Use “Send to library” to move them only when you choose. Keep cloud files downloaded if you continue seeding them.

Goodreads & browsers

Your browser keeps your sign-in session.

Connect Goodreads on Discover for automatic shelf refresh using its saved Goodreads session. Other links open in the browser selected above.

Safari import reads only the active Goodreads book or shelf page after you allow Automation access. Other browsers can send a book using the bookmarklet or by pasting its link. Imports are read-only; local listening progress does not change Goodreads ratings.

○

Connection privacy

listen. sends downloads to your client; the client controls torrent networking.

A torrent proxy is not a VPN, and no configuration is guaranteed leak-proof. listen. does not change your system proxy, VPN, DNS, or firewall. Catalog and Goodreads browsing use their own network connections.
